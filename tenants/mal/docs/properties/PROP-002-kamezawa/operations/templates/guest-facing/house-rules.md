## For our neighbors

Japanese residential streets are very close to homes. Loud conversations, groups on the street late at night, or slamming doors can disturb families next door. If you would not do it at home after 10 PM, please do not do it here.

---

## Trash & recycling

Japan separates waste strictly. **Please do not leave bags on the street or at public collection points** — that violates local rules and bothers neighbors.

1. Use the **labeled bags/bins in the kitchen**
2. Separate roughly as follows:
   - **Burnable** — food scraps, paper, wrappers (seal wet waste)
   - **Non-burnable** — ceramics, glass (non-bottle), small hard items
   - **Recyclables** — PET bottles, cans, glass bottles (rinse lightly)
3. **Before checkout:** place sorted waste in the kitchen bins only
4. **Large items:** message us — do not abandon furniture or oversized trash

We dispose on the proper collection days. Thank you for sorting.

## During your stay

- Lock doors and windows when you go out
- Turn off **AC, lights, and gas** when leaving
- Do not share the door code with anyone outside your group
- Report damage or broken items promptly — accidents happen
- **No open flames** — candles, fireworks, charcoal indoors
