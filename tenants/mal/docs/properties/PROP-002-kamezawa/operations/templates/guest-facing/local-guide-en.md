# Local Guide — Kamezawa & Ryogoku

**For international guests · Kamezawa House, Sumida-ku, Tokyo**

---

## Where you are

You are staying in **Kamezawa**, a historic neighborhood in **Sumida Ward** — the birthplace of **Katsushika Hokusai** (1760), Japan’s most famous ukiyo-e artist (*The Great Wave*). The area mixes quiet residential streets with museums, sumo culture, and easy access to **Tokyo Skytree** and **Asakusa**.

## From the station to the house

1. Exit **Ryogoku Station** (Oedo Line) at **A3 or A4**
2. Cross the street to **Hokusai-dori** (you will see the Edo-yu bathhouse sign nearby at Kamezawa 1-5-8)
3. Walk along the residential side — the house is **about 1 minute** from the exit  
   *Exact door location is in your check-in message.*

**JR Ryogoku Station** (Sobu Line) is also available — **~5–8 min walk** via the pedestrian bridge or Hokusai-dori.

## Half-day & day trips

### Route A — Skytree & Solamachi (**~20 min walk** or 1 train stop)

Walk north along the Sumida River, or take **Oedo Line** Ryogoku → **Ryogoku** (1 min) / walk to **Tokyo Skytree** area.

- **Tokyo Skytree** — observation decks, shopping mall **Solamachi**
- **Sumida Aquarium** — at Skytree base
- **Tokyo Mizumachi** — shops under the railway near the river

### Route B — Asakusa & Senso-ji (**~25–30 min walk** or **~5 min train**)

- Walk west across the Sumida area, or **Oedo Line** Ryogoku → **Kuramae** / **Asakusa**
- **Senso-ji Temple**, **Kaminarimon Gate**, **Nakamise** shopping street
- Continue to **Sumida River Walk** → Skytree (scenic **~15–20 min** walk)

## Culture notes

- **Trash:** Never leave bags outside the house — use kitchen bins only ([House Rules](house-rules.md))
- **Bathing:** Japanese bathrooms — shower **before** entering the tub; towel stays outside the tub
- **Slippers:** Indoor slippers may be provided; toilet slippers are separate if available
- **Sumo:** When tournaments run at Kokugikan, the area gets busy — book restaurants early
