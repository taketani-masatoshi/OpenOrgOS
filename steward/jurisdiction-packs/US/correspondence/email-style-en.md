# English (US) external email — style stub

**Locale:** `en-US` · **Jurisdiction:** US  
**Canonical contract:** [steward/core/correspondence/style-contract.md](../../../core/correspondence/style-contract.md)  
**Machine rules:** [style.yaml](style.yaml)

> Stub pack. Prefer short, direct English. Recipient locale wins over sender jurisdiction when `correspondence_locale` is set on the contact.

## Required shape

1. Addressee (`Dear {Name},`)
2. Purpose first (no long seasonal openers)
3. Facts only (datetime, venue or join URL)
4. Short closing + company signature

## Forbidden

- Explaining the technical "from" address
- Inventing relationship labels
- Marketing tone for venue suggestions
