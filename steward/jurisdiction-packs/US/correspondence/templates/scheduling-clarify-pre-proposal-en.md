# Template: venue options before proposing dates (in-person)

**Use:** Clarify email **before** date/time proposals. No datetime or allergy lines.  
**Canonical JA:** [scheduling-clarify-pre-proposal-ja.md](../../JP/correspondence/templates/scheduling-clarify-pre-proposal-ja.md)

```
Dear {full_name},

I am writing on behalf of {company}.

We would like to arrange a meeting regarding {purpose_confirmed}.

Please let us know your preferred venue. We will follow up with date proposals separately.

Area: {area}
{cost_line}

【Venue options】 (please let us know your preference; we will arrange)
A. {venue_a}{venue_a_facts_suffix}
B. {venue_b}{venue_b_facts_suffix}
C. {venue_c}{venue_c_facts_suffix}

Thank you,

{company}
```

### Rules

- `purpose_confirmed` must be CEO-confirmed wording only
- Facts only in `venue_*_facts_suffix` (private room, walking minutes) — no sales copy
- **Do not** include date/time slots or allergy questions (allergy lives in counterparty DB)
